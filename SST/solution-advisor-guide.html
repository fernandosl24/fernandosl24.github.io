What the full track should cover

Across the full track, make sure Sales and CSM receive the six elements below. One block does not need to cover everything.

Solution overview

Explain what the solution is, which customer problem it helps with and where it fits in the SAP portfolio. Keep it simple enough that Sales and CSM can explain the main idea later.

Business value

Explain the customer value. What can improve for the customer? Which priorities, KPIs, risks, costs or growth opportunities can the solution support?

Competitive positioning

Give enough context about competition and explain where SAP is different. Focus on the points that are useful in a customer conversation.

Licensing / commercial basics

Cover the licensing and commercial basics that are useful for Sales and CSM. This is not a detailed pricing session.

Resources

Show where they can find more information later, such as internal pages, learning content, demo resources, battlecards and useful communities.

Demo or interactive experience

Whenever possible, show rather than only tell. This can be a live demo, a guided demo, a hands on exercise, a mini workshop, a customer scenario, or another interactive moment that helps the audience apply what they just learned.

Adjust the depth to your block.

Use the time you have based on your topic. You do not need the same level of detail in every session.

If you have a short block

  • Prioritize the overview, business value and one practical example.
  • Point to licensing, competition and resources without spending too much time on details.
  • Use one focused demo if it helps the topic.

If you have a 45 to 60 minute block

  • Use the extra time for a demo, scenario, exercise, hands on activity or Q&A.
  • Keep the interaction connected to the topic.
  • Check with the other presenters so the same content is not repeated.
